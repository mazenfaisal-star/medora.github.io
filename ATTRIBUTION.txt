Cogni Anatomy describes its atlas as an interactive browser-based viewer with selectable anatomical structures, system visibility controls, zoom/rotation, isolation/transparency and section tools. It uses multiple datasets and licenses; where content includes Z-Anatomy geometry, CC BY-SA 4.0 requirements apply. See https://cogni.cards/anatomy/about/ for source-specific license details and limitations. The viewer models are educational reference anatomy, not diagnostic tools and not representations of individual variation.

The existing Medora primary models remain in place: the Sketchfab brain, the existing heart/lung GLB reference models and the user's Ebers male reproductive system Sketchfab embed. The male atlas was moved out of its collapsed details disclosure so it appears visibly below the primary male-system model, matching the placement used on the other organ pages.
